Privacy & safety.

See how your messages are handled, manage your identity, or sign out from this device.

On-device encryption
⛨

Your messages are encrypted before upload.

Vertex uses browser cryptography to encrypt message content and media. Supabase stores ciphertext and wrapped message keys, not readable message text.

✦ Message encryptionEach message uses a random AES-GCM key. That key is wrapped separately for you and your friend with their public encryption keys.
⌁ Password-protected keyYour private key backup is encrypted using a key derived from your account password. A lost password can mean lost message access.
◷ Message retentionMessages have no automatic expiry. A sender can permanently delete their own message; deletion removes it for both people.
♢ Media and giftsVoice/video note files are encrypted before upload. Song previews come from the linked music provider and need an internet connection.
